# Before/After Examples

Из stop-slop + Zak's review cases.

---

## 1. Throat-Clearing + Binary Contrast

**Before:**
> "Here's the thing: building products is hard. Not because the technology is complex. Because people are complex. Let that sink in."

**After:**
> "Building products is hard. Technology is manageable. People aren't."

---

## 2. Filler + Permission-Granting

**Before:**
> "It turns out that most teams struggle with alignment. The uncomfortable truth is that nobody wants to admit they're confused. And that's okay."

**After:**
> "Most teams struggle with alignment, and people rarely say out loud that they're confused."

---

## 3. Business Jargon Stack

**Before:**
> "In today's fast-paced landscape, we need to lean into discomfort and navigate uncertainty with clarity."

**After:**
> "We need to make decisions with less information than we'd like."

(Исходник ничего не утверждает, кроме этого. Итог короче, и это правильно: новых утверждений, вроде «конкуренты быстрее», не добавляем.)

---

## 4. Dramatic Fragmentation

**Before:**
> "Speed. Quality. Cost. You can only pick two. That's it. That's the tradeoff."

**After:**
> "Speed, quality, cost, pick two."

---

## 5. Rhetorical Setup

**Before:**
> "What if I told you that the best teams don't optimize for productivity? Here's what I mean: they optimize for learning. Think about it."

**After:**
> "The best teams optimize for learning."

---

## 6. Closing-Question Template (Zak rule 3)

**Before:**
> "I'd love to hear how others approached this. Was it more like a clear moment, or more of a slow drift? And did you bring it up or did your prescriber?"

**After:**
> "the part i still don't have a handle on is whether the drift was the medication doing its job, or whether the medication was over and i hadn't clocked it. nobody i've talked to about it has the same answer"

---

## 7. "For people who…" opener (Zak rule 4)

**Before:**
> "For people who tried bupropion after SSRI, how did you decide?"

**After:**
> "how does the SSRI-to-bupropion switch decision actually get made"

или

> "i keep wondering how people end up on bupropion instead of staying on an SSRI"

---

## 8. Outsider-Disclosure Opener (Zak rule 6)

**Before:**
> "I'm posting this from outside. I was treated for depression, was on an SSRI for about two years, tapered off a few months ago. No bipolar history, nothing in my experience that overlaps with what this sub is primarily about. The thing I keep not being able to picture is the diagnosis flip..."

**After:**
> "The thing I keep not being able to picture is the diagnosis flip. A lot of posts here describe a point when their depression diagnosis got revised to bipolar or bp2, and the way they describe it sounds less like a correction and more like a reframe of years.
>
> Coming at this from the depression side, I'd been on an SSRI for two years, off now..."

---

## 9. Scientific Jargon in Casual Post (Zak rule 5)

**Before:**
> "I tracked my sleep for 6 months. Sample size of 1, so take with a grain of salt. The methodology was self-reported via Notion. Confirmation bias is real, but the patterns I saw were statistically significant."

**After:**
> "I tracked my sleep for 6 months. Just my experience, could be wrong. Wrote it down in Notion every morning. Might be reading too much into it, but the patterns are real enough that I started changing things."

---

## 10. Russian Throat-Clearing + Vague Declarative

**Before:**
> "На самом деле, если честно, дело в том, что причины здесь глубже. Запомните это. Это меняет всё."

**After:**
> "Причина в том, что [конкретная причина]. Это меняет [конкретное что]."

---

## 11. Em-dashes everywhere

**Before:**
> "The product — the one we shipped last week — wasn't perfect — but it solved the core problem — and that's what mattered."

**After:**
> "The product we shipped last week wasn't perfect, but it solved the core problem."

(Мораль «That's what mattered» убрана: абзац кончается вместе с содержанием.)

---

## 12. Quotes-for-emphasis (Zak rule 2)

**Before:**
> "I'd write things like \"felt off today\" or \"too much\" and at the time those entries felt like data, but reading them back they're \"feelings disguised as observations.\""

**After:**
> "I'd write things like felt off today or too much, and at the time those entries felt like data. Reading them back they're feelings disguised as observations."
